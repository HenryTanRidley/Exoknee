The final version of exoknee mechanical design. Most parts are 3D printed with nylon. Screws and springs for brakes are not included. The brakes are made with copper.

The lengths of brake1，2，3 need to be adjusted for best braking effect since there is dimensional uncertainty in 3D-printed parts.

All gears are off-the-shelf copper spur gears with m=2. Other components purchased online:
1. PP bearings x3, for motor pulleys
2. steel bearings x2, for cam axis
3. steel bearings x1, for idler gear
4. PU cords
5. carbon fiber tubings x 4
6. servo motor, HMU-B50
7. K-Tech BLDC with gearbox and encoder

See Exoknee_BOM file for a complete list.
