The final version of exoknee mechanical design. Most parts are 3D printed with nylon. Screws and springs for brakes are not included. All gears are off-the-shelf copper spur gears with m=2. Other components purchased online:
1. PP bearings x3, for motor pulleys
2. steel bearings x2, for cam axis
3. steel bearings x1, for idler gear
4. A85 PU cords
5. carbon fiber tubings x 4
